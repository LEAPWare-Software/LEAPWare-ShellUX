Verdict: DISAGREE

The rule change itself is correct and well tested. I could not break the fail-closed logic, find a leak into WARN, or make the rule raise from attacker-controlled input. I am disagreeing because of one confirmed defect in the named defect class (finding 1). The PR changed what the hook's deny output means but did not touch `plugins/claude/lwb/bin/lwb_hook.py` or `adapters/claude/hook_io.py`.    - **Fuzzing.** 60,000 direct `evaluate()` calls across DENY and WARN, with random printable, quote, heredoc, `\udcff` and non-ASCII commands, random branch strings, random proof ids, and `repo=None` or `facts_incomplete` → `fuzz calls: 60000 raised: 0`.
